# 3-minute demo video — script

Record at 1440×900, web app or Mac app. Keep the cursor slow. Times are targets.

**0:00–0:15 — The problem.** Title card: "Thousands of papers are retracted every year for reused images, made-up data
and invented references. Most were caught by volunteers, by hand." Show a mirrored micrograph pair blinking.

**0:15–0:40 — Paste a DOI.** Home screen. Paste a DOI of an open-access paper. Narrate the progress line: "It
fetches the paper, the original figure files and every supplementary file from PubMed Central and the publisher."

**0:40–1:20 — The demo paper (planted problems, so no real author is accused).** Click "Try the demo paper".
* Findings list: integrity signals vs quality notes; category chips.
* Click the mirrored duplicate → the **blink comparator**: "Two panels labelled as different treatments. Aligned and
  flickered, nothing moves except brightness: it is one image, mirrored." Switch to Difference (black = identical).
* Click the overlapping fields → Swipe mode.
* Click the cloned region → the arrow between two identical patches. Switch the stage to **Reveal** and **Noise**.
* Click the spliced blot → the seam line.

**1:20–1:50 — Numbers and text.** Click: p-value mismatch ("t(14) = 2.10 cannot give p < 0.001"), impossible mean
(GRIM), the same SE after every mean, chatbot text next to "no generative AI was used", tortured phrases
("counterfeit consciousness" = artificial intelligence).

**1:50–2:15 — Supplementary data.** Drop a PDF plus an .xlsx: copied block of values (cells shown), a `RAND()`
formula, gene names turned into dates, a hidden sheet. "Spreadsheets nobody opens."

**2:15–2:35 — References and record (real DOI).** Paper tab: self-citation rate, retracted references, references
that cannot be found, authors' retractions, files examined.

**2:35–2:55 — AI investigator.** Click "Ask the AI investigator": it opens the evidence, reads the legend, marks a
finding "confirmed" and another "false positive", and drafts the comment. Show the draft asking for raw data.

**2:55–3:00 — Close.** "Paper Inspector: a second look for every paper. Candidates, not verdicts."

Notes: never show a real paper's finding as "fraud" — use the demo paper for the manipulation walkthrough, and for real
DOIs show only the Paper tab or a retracted paper's already-public retraction.
